Tired of losing your tabs in a messy horizontal strip?

TAB Explorer is the desktop-grade file manager for Google Chrome. It lives right inside Chrome’s side panel, organizing your open tabs and research into Workspaces and color-coded Folders with batch reopening, crash recovery, and zero cloud tracking.

Real Workspaces & Nested Folders

Separate your client projects, research topics, and daily reading into independent workspaces. Nest folders infinitely, star priorities, and drag-and-drop links with ease.

⚡

Non-Blocking Batch Reopening

Open an entire folder of 30+ tabs without your computer stuttering. TAB Explorer spawns tabs in throttled increments, keeping Chrome silky smooth and responsive.

Crash Recovery & 50-Step Undo

Accidentally closed a folder or suffered a browser crash? TAB Explorer automatically detects lost sessions for 1-click restore, plus gives you up to 50 persistent Ctrl+Z undo steps.
